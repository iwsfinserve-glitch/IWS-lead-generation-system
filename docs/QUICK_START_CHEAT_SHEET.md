## ⚡ 2. The 15-Minute Daily Routine

```markdown
1. [09:00 AM] 🔔 Check Notification Bell (Top Right)
   ↳ View pending appointments, overdue follow-ups, and approval notifications.

2. [09:15 AM] 📥 Check 'Unassigned' Tab in Leads (/leads)
   ↳ Click "Claim Lead" on incoming fresh prospects.

3. [10:00 AM] 🧠 Check Gemini AI Insights
   ↳ Open your assigned leads and review AI Win Probability & Recommended Talking Points.

4. [11:00 AM] 📞 Log Client Calls & Notes
   ↳ Always type notes in the interaction box immediately after a phone or video call.

5. [02:00 PM] 💬 Send WhatsApp Follow-Ups
   ↳ Use pre-formatted greeting and brochure templates from the WhatsApp Inbox or Widget.

6. [05:00 PM] 📅 Schedule Follow-Up Appointments
   ↳ Set up consultations with automatic Google Calendar sync.
```

## 🎯 3. Lead Stages & Progression Rules

```
[Unassigned] ──► [In Progress] ──► [Potential] ──► [Converted to Investor]
                       │                 │
                       └──► [Non-Potential]
```

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Stage Update Rule**:
> When a lead is ready to move to **Potential**, **Converted**, or **Non-Potential**, click **Request Status Change** on the lead page and enter brief notes. Your Sales Manager will approve it with one click!

---

## 💬 4. WhatsApp & Google Calendar Shortcuts

- **Floating WhatsApp Widget**: Click the green WhatsApp icon in the bottom-right of any screen to chat without losing your place.
- **Google Calendar Sync**: Go to `/appointments` and click **Connect Google Calendar** to automatically sync all meetings to your phone calendar.
- **Exporting Your Leads**: Go to `/leads` ➔ click **My Clients** tab ➔ click **Export CSV** in top right.
